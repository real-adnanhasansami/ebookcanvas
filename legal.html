About

Ebook Canvas turns uploaded PDFs into a page-turning reading experience, with an optional audiobook mode and a focus mode for distraction-free reading. Books are added by the Ebook Canvas team; readers browse, read, and listen.

Terms of use

By creating an account you agree to use Ebook Canvas for personal reading only. Books remain the property of their rights holders; copying, redistributing, or downloading book content outside the app isn't permitted. Premium subscriptions are arranged manually and confirmed by our team - see the Refunds section below for cancellations.

Privacy

We store your account details (name, email), reading progress, and subscription status to run the service. We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, data storage, and basic usage analytics. We don't sell your data. You can request full account deletion at any time from your account page.

Refunds & cancellations

Because subscriptions are activated manually, cancellations and refund requests are handled the same way - message us on WhatsApp or email and we'll sort it out directly, usually within a few hours.
